Brindis Bistro & Bar (Brindis Mediterranean Cuisine) is a wood-fired Mediterranean and Turkish restaurant and bar at 180 W Continental Rd #178 in Green Valley, Arizona. Our 900-degree brick oven turns out hand-stretched pides, lahmacun, shish kebabs, mezze with tzatziki and grilled pita, and baklava. By day we serve quick wood-fired pide lunches on our large misted patio; by night, a full Mediterranean dinner with wine, house sangria, live music, indoor dining and a cozy bar. Open Tuesday through Sunday from 11am — early dinner welcome. Order online, dine in, or call 520-849-6188. See the full menu, our story, live music nights, and directions and reservations.
