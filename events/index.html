After Dark · Green Valley

Live Music
on the Patio

When the desert cools, the patio fills. Glasses pour, the music starts, and dinner becomes an evening — under the misters and the stars.

Jazz, country and acoustic sets are a regular part of evenings at Brindis — local players on the misted patio, house sangria and Mediterranean reds at the bar, and the woodfire going behind it all.

The lineup changes with the season (and snowbird season fills up fast). For this week’s schedule, give us a call at 520-849-6188 or follow @brindismediterranean on Instagram — we post who’s playing and when.

Planning a birthday, club lunch or a bigger gathering? We take reservations for parties of six or more, and the patio is made for groups. Get in touch and we’ll set the table.
