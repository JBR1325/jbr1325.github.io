Our Story

From Anatolia to the
Sonoran Desert

A family-owned kitchen in Green Valley, built around one thing: a wood-fired brick oven that never cools.

Brindis opened in 2024 in Continental Shopping Plaza as a neighborhood bistro — and found its true voice when the kitchen turned fully toward the wood-fired Turkish and eastern Mediterranean cooking of its roots. The menu changed. The heart of the room never did: the brick oven you see the moment you walk in, burning at around 900 degrees.

Nearly everything hot in the house meets that flame. Hand-stretched pide blisters in about ninety seconds. Lahmacun comes out thin and crackling under spiced beef, parsley and lemon. Kebabs char over the fire; the lamb shank braises slowly until it falls from the bone; whole sea bass roasts with crisped skin. The char is the seasoning — savory, never bitter.

The room stays easy: wooden tables, a little bar pouring wine, beer and our house sangria, and a big misted patio under umbrellas and string lights where Green Valley lingers through the evening — often to live music.

We’re proud to be a family-owned table in this community — close to home for Green Valley and Sahuarita, and worth the drive from Tucson. Come early, stay late; the oven is at full heat all afternoon.
