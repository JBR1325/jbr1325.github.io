Good to Know

Do you take reservations?

We take reservations for parties of six or more — call 520-849-6188. Smaller groups are welcome to walk right in.

What are your hours?

Tuesday through Saturday 11am–8pm, Sunday 11am–6pm, closed Mondays. Early dinner is welcome — the oven is at full heat all afternoon. See hours.

Do you have outdoor seating?

Yes — a large patio with misters, shade umbrellas and string lights. A favorite for lunch, and for evenings with live music.

Do you have vegetarian or vegan options?

Plenty. Most of our mezze is vegan (hummus, baba ghanoush, dolmas, tabbouleh), and the spinach & feta, veggie and cheese pides, salads and Turkish pasta are vegetarian. Items are tagged VG and VT on the menu.

Can I order takeout online?

Yes — order online through Toast, or call 520-849-6188 and we’ll have it ready.

Do you have live music?

Yes — jazz, country and acoustic sets are a regular part of evenings here. Call or check @brindismediterranean for this week’s lineup.

Where do I park?

We’re in Continental Shopping Plaza at 180 W Continental Rd, Suite 178 — plaza parking is steps from the door.

Do you serve alcohol?

Yes — wine, beer and our house sangria, poured at the little bar or brought to your table.
